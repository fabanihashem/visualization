      **Why it’s good:**
       1. **Low cognitive load** – The line graph cleanly shows trends over time using a 2D layout; the viewer can easily discern changes without clutter or extra effects :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}.

    - How could this data visualization have been improved?

       **Improvements for good:**
       1. **Color accessibility** – Ensure color choices are friendly to those with color vision deficiencies (e.g., blue vs orange instead of blue vs pink).
       2. **Add contextual annotations** – Highlight key events (e.g., economic crises, COVID-19) directly on the chart—this adds narrative without distracting from the substantive data.

       **How to improve for bad:**
      1. **Switch to bar chart** – Replace with a sorted bar chart so viewers can reliably compare lengths – a design principle supported in our course and Chart Catalog :contentReference[oaicite:10]{index=10}.
